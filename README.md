Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Famous Last Words at 1 Percent"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-famous-last-words-at-1-percent-63e2b0b4) — the funniest last words a phone says when its battery hits 1 percent, as a single line of at most 199 characters.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I survived four hundred drops, one toilet, and your toddler — but one percent is what kills me. Delete my browser history.

Run it with Node 22.18+:

```sh
node main.ts
```
